Privacy policy

Effective October 23, 2026

Hearth is a private, self-hosted family wall calendar. It runs on a Raspberry Pi in our home and is used only by members of our household. This policy explains what Hearth does with information it receives from Google when a household member connects their Google account.

What Hearth accesses

  • Your Google Calendar events, read-only (scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.readonly): the list of calendars you can see, and each event’s title, time, location, description and colour.
  • Your email address (scopes openid and userinfo.email), used only to label the connected account in Hearth’s settings.

Hearth does not request permission to create, change or delete events, and it never does so.

How the information is used

Hearth uses calendar data solely to display your events on our family’s wall calendar and on our own phones and computers at home — including using each event’s colour to decide which family member’s column it appears in. It is not used for advertising, profiling, analytics, or any other purpose.

Where it is stored

  • All data is stored only on our own device (a Raspberry Pi on our home network). There is no Hearth cloud service.
  • Google sign-in tokens are stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM). Event data is kept for a rolling window of about 60 days back and 13 months ahead and is refreshed every few minutes.
  • The device is not exposed to the public internet; remote access, if used, is limited to our household’s private network.

Sharing

Hearth does not sell, rent, share or transfer Google user data to anyone. No third parties, advertisers or analytics providers receive it, and it is not used to train any AI or machine-learning models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Hearth’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and deleting data

  • In Hearth: Admin → Calendars → Google accounts → Disconnect. This revokes Hearth’s access with Google and deletes that account’s stored tokens and calendar events from the device.
  • At any time from Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions → Hearth → Remove access.

Children

Hearth is operated by parents for their own household. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone outside the household.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
