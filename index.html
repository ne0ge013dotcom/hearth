Hearth

A private family wall calendar, running on our own Raspberry Pi at home.

Hearth shows our family’s calendars, chores, notes and smart-home controls on a touchscreen in the kitchen. It is a personal, self-hosted project. It is not a public service, it has no user accounts for anyone outside our household, and it does not collect, sell or share anyone’s data.

How it uses Google

When a family member chooses Connect Google account, Hearth asks Google for read-only access to their calendars (calendar.readonly) and their email address (to label the account). It uses that access only to show their events — and the colour each event was given — on our wall calendar. It never creates, edits or deletes events, and the data stays on our own device.

  • Where it runs: a Raspberry Pi on our home network
  • Google access: read-only calendar + email address
  • Shared with: no one
  • Ads / analytics / tracking: none
